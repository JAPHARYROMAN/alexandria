# Alexandria founding terms

## Scope and authority

Alexandria is a global, open-source knowledge repository with a decentralized official network. Its founder is the sole owner of the official platform. The owner reserves the final decision over scope, official releases, software licence, policy, budget, roadmap, official domains, appointments and network admission. Editors and operators act under delegations the owner can revoke. An independent custodian is optional in the distant future.

The full public release includes the Library, Universal Search, the Global Knowledge Crawler, the Media Centre, source discussions, the Academy, platform reviews and the in-app Community Forum. Readers may explore accepted and refuted claims, beliefs, historical accounts and predictions. Each assessment should identify its author, date, evidence and uncertainty. Preserve original languages and credit translations. Internal milestones precede the owner’s public release decision.

## Code and collections

The project's software is licensed under Apache-2.0. The repository includes the licence text. The manifest records separate permissions for the seed material. Developers may propose patches with tests and provenance. The owner or an appointed maintainer reviews changes. Only an owner-authorized release is official. Under the selected open-source licence developers may fork and self-host. A separate installation must identify its operator and requires the owner’s authorization to join the official network.

Each source has its own terms. Record the title, credit as supplied, original language, basis for holding the item, and permitted audience and uses. Creators retain control over permissions they can grant. Online availability alone is insufficient to copy a work. Reading the Sky collects observations, explanations and historical interpretations. The General Reading Room spans disciplines, traditions, testimony and methods of inquiry.

## Working with a source

A reader opens a collection, finds a source, sees its credit and use terms, and can follow the origin. A creator submits their work, chooses credit and permissions, and can request correction or withdrawal. An editor checks the origin, consent and scope, records a decision and refers unresolved rights to the owner. Preserve differing attributed accounts and attach evidence to any editorial assessment. An owner authorizes the first source, official releases and changes to these terms.

On a credible rights or consent challenge, the assigned editor restricts the affected material pending review. Record the request, decision, reason and appeal privately where needed. A different assigned reviewer considers an appeal, with the owner holding final platform authority. For withdrawal, remove affected bodies, previews and derived indexes from official serving paths, propagate the restriction and retain only a permitted notice. Review restored or rejoining nodes before serving them. Prior recipients may retain copies under the permissions previously granted.

Moderators handle harassment, exposure of private information and false attribution. They record the reason, notify the contributor where possible and offer an appeal. A disputed claim can remain discoverable alongside its sources and counterevidence.

## Official nodes

The proposed network starts with two owner-operated nodes in separate hosting environments. Both use the same signed official changes, stable source identifiers and current permissions. A reviewed reusable source can travel with its credit, origin and terms. A link-only record carries Alexandria’s public description and link. Private originals, pending submissions and withdrawn records stay outside replication. A restriction takes precedence over an older grant. Phase 1 emits a replication plan for inspection. Phase 2 delivers transport, change ordering, recovery and enforcement at the serving boundary.

## Community direction after full launch

A reader can submit a platform review in their language or open a proposal in the in-app Community Forum. Moderators route source disputes to the relevant source, group duplicate proposals and retain links to the original feedback. Participants discuss trade-offs and register support. Editors summarize evidence, minority-language requests and recurring needs. The owner publishes an accept, defer or decline decision with reasons. An accepted proposal links to its release note when shipped. Votes inform the decision. They confer no release authority.

## Founding approval

The owner may decide directly or delegate the decision. Record the date, decision maker, basis of authority and digest of the exact founding packet. A detached signature may also be verified against a separately trusted owner key. A material change requires a fresh decision. Approval closes Phase 1 and authorizes work on the repository foundation. The owner retains authority over the public platform launch.
